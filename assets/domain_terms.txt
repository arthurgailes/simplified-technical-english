# Technical nouns admitted under ASD-STE100 rules 1.5, 1.6, and 1.8.
#
# STE was written for aircraft maintenance manuals, so its dictionary has no
# vocabulary for housing, finance, or statistics. Rule 1.6 permits words that
# are not in the dictionary when they are technical nouns of your subject
# field, and rule 1.8 says to take those from your company or industry
# terminology. This file is that terminology list.
#
# One term per line, lowercase. Multi-word terms are matched as phrases.
# Blank lines and lines starting with # are ignored.
# Add to it rather than mangling a term into approved words: renaming a
# technical noun breaks rule 1.11 (do not use different nouns for the same
# item) and usually makes the text less accurate, not more readable.
#
# Each section below cites the categories of technical noun that rule 1.5
# enumerates, so every term has a stated justification rather than resting on
# "it sounds technical". The full list of 22 categories is in
# references/rules/section-1-words.md. Where a term is subject-field
# vocabulary the standard's categories do not enumerate, rule 1.8 covers it:
# take technical nouns from your industry's terminology.
#
# Some terms here collide with a word the dictionary disallows in another
# sense -- "permit" against LET (v), "spread" against APPLY (v). Those are kept
# deliberately, and the checker reports each use as an advisory naming both
# senses rather than silently allowing it. List them with:
#   python scripts/ste_check.py --audit-domain-terms
